
## Supply Chain Analysis 
📌 Project Overview

This project presents an end-to-end Supply Chain Analysis using SQL, Excel, and Power BI. The goal is to analyze supply chain performance by evaluating costs, revenue, profit margins, supplier efficiency, lead times, shipping costs, and defect rates through an interactive dashboard.

The dashboard enables stakeholders to monitor key supply chain KPIs and make data-driven decisions.

 ## Objective

The objective of this project is to analyze supply chain data to:

Monitor total cost and revenue by product category

Evaluate profit and profit margins

Analyze supplier performance, lead times, and defect rates

Understand shipping costs and logistics efficiency

Support data-driven operational and strategic decisions


### Tools & Technologies

SQL – Data extraction, joins, aggregations, KPI calculations

Excel – Data cleaning, preprocessing, and validation

Power BI – Interactive dashboard creation and visualization




## Dataset Description

The dataset includes supply chain information such as:

Product categories (Cosmetics, Haircare, Skincare)

Cost and revenue details

Supplier data

Shipping carriers and shipping time

Lead times and defect rates

City-wise and product-wise data




##       Key KPIs in Dashboard

Total Cost & Total Revenue

Profit & Profit Margin

Cost and Revenue by Product Type

Average Lead Time & Shipping Time

Supplier-wise Defect Rates

Shipping Cost by Carrier

City-wise and Product-wise filtering



##         Key Insights

Skincare generates the highest revenue among product categories

Profit margins remain consistently high across all product types

Certain suppliers show higher defect rates and longer lead times

Shipping costs vary significantly across carriers

Lead time optimization can improve overall supply chain efficiency




##      Business Recommendations

Focus on high-performing product categories for revenue growth

Optimize supplier selection based on defect rates and lead times

Improve logistics planning to reduce shipping and lead times

Monitor shipping carriers to control logistics costs

Use dashboards regularly for performance tracking
